# Bundled fonts

- **Inter** (variable, latin + latin-ext) — © The Inter Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **Playfair Display** (variable, latin + latin-ext) — © The Playfair Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Files come from the `@fontsource-variable/inter` and `@fontsource-variable/playfair-display`
packages (5.2.5) and are served by the app itself; no request ever leaves for a font CDN.
The OFL permits bundling and redistribution with software; the fonts are not sold on their own.
